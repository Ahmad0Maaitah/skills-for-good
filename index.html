HOW IT WORKS

A simple idea.
A meaningful exchange.

01↗
◇

Receive a voucher

Participants receive an educational NFT voucher transferred to their own wallet on Polygon.

02↗
◎

Unlock the lesson

Connect a wallet. The website verifies ownership of the correct token before unlocking full learning access.

03↗
✳

Learn and practise

Work through lessons, interactive money calculators, scenario questions and a final assessment.

04↗
♡

Turn learning into impact

Arrange volunteer mentoring. A completed session is recorded separately from the NFT transfer.

i
Why we separate the NFT from the outcome

An on-chain transfer demonstrates token movement—not that anyone attended a lesson. Real impact requires separate confirmation of services actually delivered.
